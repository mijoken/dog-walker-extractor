# v0.2.0

## Status

Official frozen dog-walker association baseline.

## Scoring change from v0.1

Only the duration-factor saturation constant changed.

v0.1:

`sqrt(common_span_sec / 3.0)`

v0.2:

`sqrt(common_span_sec / 2.5)`

The independent persistence term remains unchanged:

`min(1.0, common_span_sec / 3.0)`

Event thresholds remain unchanged:

- minimum pair score: 0.55
- minimum pair span: 1.0 s
- minimum dog frames: 5
- event merge gap: 1.5 s

## Development evidence

The 2.5-second value was selected after examining the short-duration
false negative in Blind Validation 002.

Therefore Validations 001-003 are treated as development/regression
evidence for this parameter rather than independent confirmation.

## Independent Blind Validation 004

A new unseen approximately 66-minute source video was processed before
human review.

Human review of the generated coarse-candidate windows identified four
distinct dog-walking passages.

- v0.1 detected at least one final event in 3/4 passages.
- v0.2 detected at least one final event in 4/4 passages.
- Neither version produced a final dog-walker event in the seven
  reviewed human-negative candidate windows.

The additional v0.2 detection was Candidate 001:

- event interval: 04:23.57 - 04:24.87
- person track: 84
- dog track: 124
- association span: 1.30 s
- dog detection frames: 40
- mean dog confidence: approximately 0.799
- v0.2 pair score: 0.551

Human review confirmed this event was a real dog walker.

## Scope limitation

Validation 004 was reviewed at the generated-candidate-window level.

The full approximately 66-minute video was not exhaustively annotated
frame-by-frame.

Accordingly, Validation 004 supports improved short-duration
person-dog association among generated candidates, but it does not
establish definitive whole-video recall.

## Lighting audit

Candidates 001-013 had broadly similar global luminance despite large
differences in dog-track stability.

Candidate 014 at approximately 57 minutes was substantially darker:

- mean luminance: 95.66
- median luminance: 77.40
- dark-pixel ratio below 64: 0.385

This indicates that the earlier tracking-quality differences cannot
primarily be attributed to overall scene brightness.

Very-low-light and true night performance remain separate future
validation topics.
